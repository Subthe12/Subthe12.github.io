Introduction


Millions of years ago, Earth was a very different place. Great apes were the dominant creatures. Over time, these apes evolved into hominids. these hominids evolved into homo habilis, into homo erectus into homo sapiens.

Early humans roamed from one place to another in search of food and shelter. Some lived in trees, which provided protection from wild animals. Some also lived in caves. It provided more protection against heat and cold but offered less protection against wild animals.

Introduction


Millions of years ago, Earth was a very different place. Great apes were the dominant creatures. Over time, these apes evolved into hominids. these hominids evolved into homo habilis, into homo erectus into homo sapiens.

Shelter

Early humans roamed from one place to another in search of food and shelter. Some lived in trees, which protected wild animals. Some also lived in caves. It provided more protection against heat and cold but offered less protection against wild animals.

Food

We know that birds and animals move from place to place in search of food. Early people also followed the same pattern. Their main diet consisted of fruits, nuts, roots, honey, and other vegetables they found growing in the wild. Early humans also hunted animals like deer, elephants, giraffes, and buffalo for food and clothing.

Introduction


Millions of years ago, our Earth had a very different environment. Great apes ruled the world at that time. Slowly, the apes evolved into hominids. Then these hominids evolved into homo habilis, into homo erectus into homo sapiens.

██████████████████

Early humans roamed from one place to another in search of food and shelter. Some lived in trees, and trees sheltered them from wild animals. Some also lived in caves. It provided more protection to heat anc cold, but offered less protection to wild animals.

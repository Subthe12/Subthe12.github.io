GEOMETRY

  • Geometry is the branch of mathematics dealing with shape, size, and relative position, forms the logical foundation of our spatial understanding.
  • The word geometry comes from 2 ancient Greek words "geo" (meaning "Earth") and "metron" (meaning "measure"). Together, they translate to "earth measurement."

In Detail

Point

In geometry, a point is an exact, zero-dimensional location in space. It has no length, width, or height. It is merely a "position" and is usually represented by a small dot on a plane and named using a capital letter.


Line

In geometry, a line is a one-dimensional, infinitely long geometric object that has no thickness or width. It extends in both the directions, and is often represented with arrowheads at both ends.


Line Segment

A line segment is a bounded portion of a straight line that links two distinct endpoints. a line segment has a fixed, measurable length and contains every point strictly between its two endpoints.


Ray

In geometry,A ray is a portion of a line that starts at a single fixed endpoint and extends infinitely in one specific direction. Unlike a line segment (which has two endpoints), a ray only goes on forever in one direction.


Angle

In geometry, An angle is a geometric figure formed when two lines, rays, or line segments meet at a common endpoint. This shared point is called the vertex, and the two lines are known as the arms or sides. Angles are typically measured in degrees (°).


Arc

In geometry, An arc is a smoothly curving line or path, to define a continuous portion of the circumference of a circle.

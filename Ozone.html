Ozone Depletion

Introduction

The Ozone Layer lies in the stratosphere between 20 and 40km above the Earth's surface. This layer protects the Earth from the Sun's ultraviolet rays. It is essential to p preserve life on the Earth.


Effects of Ozone Depletion

Affect in Human Health

It allows UV-B rays from the sun to reach the ground, which can harm our bodies by damaging the DNA in our skin cells, which leads to skin cancer.

Damage to Ocean Life

They penetrate the surface of the ocean and kill tiny floating sea plants called phytoplankton. Since they are the primary food source for small fish and sea insects, Their destruction leads to a large-scale starvation issue. his, in turn, leaves larger fish, whales, and dolphins without food, and prevents the ocean from absorbing gases that contribute to global warming.

Prevention

Planting More Trees

It will improve the level of oxygen in the atmosphere and decrease the level of oxygen in the atmosphere and decreases the level of carbon dioxide.

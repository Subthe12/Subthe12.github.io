Ozone Depletion

Introduction

The Ozone Layer lies in the stratosphere between 20km to 40km above the Earth's surface. This layer protects the Earth from the Sun's ultraviolet rays. It is essential to preserve life on Earth.


Causes of Ozone Depletion

Release of Chloroflurocarbons(CFC

The UV Rays of the sun break down CFC into chlorine atoms. Each chlorine atom combines with Oxygen atoms form chlorine monoxide, which destroys the ozone molecules.

Effects of Ozone Depletion

Effect on Human Health

It allows UV rays from the sun to reach the ground, which can harm our bodies by damaging the DNA in our skin cells, which may lead to skin cancer.

Damage to Ocean Life

The UV Rays penetrate the surface of the ocean and kill tiny floating sea plants called phytoplanktons which are the primary food source for small fish and sea insects, Their destruction leads to a large-scale starvation. This, in turn, leaves larger fish, whales, and dolphins without food.

Prevention

Planting More Trees

It will improve the level of oxygen in the atmosphere and decrease the level of oxygen in the atmosphere and decreases the level of carbon dioxide.

Use CFL (Compact Fluorescent Light)

They consume 70% less energy than ordinary bulbs.

Brief Points

Start OF Humans
Humans evolved over millions of years through a continuous process of biological evolution. We share a common ancestor with modern African apes, and we developed key survival traits like walking upright, larger brains, and tool utilisation.
Evolution
Evolution means a biological development over time. We humans, evolved from apes to hominids to Homo habilis to Homo erectus to Homo sapiens(Humans)
Periods of Evolution
The early days of humans were divided into three major periods: the Stone Age, Bronze Age, and Iron Age.
